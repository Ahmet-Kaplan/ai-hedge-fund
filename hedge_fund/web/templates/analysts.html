How to read this

Avg edge is conviction × the name's move to the next rebalance, averaged over an analyst's calls. It judges the opinion whether or not the fund acted on it. Attributed P&L is their signed share of what the fund actually held in that name, times the same move — the opinion's consequence. The two differ whenever an analyst was outvoted by their pod.

Abstentions are not neutrality. An analyst abstains when the data they need is missing, and abstentions are excluded from the blend entirely. A high abstention count means that analyst was absent from the decision, not that they were cautious — check the data coverage for the window before reading anything into their record.

Edge measures the call, not the fill. Orders execute at the next close after a decision, so realized P&L differs from these figures by a session of slippage. Built from {{ sources }} stored result{{ "" if sources == 1 else "s" }}.
