How to read this

These are the trades a deployed fund has already decided on, sized from the marks at the assessing close. The fund executes at the next close against prices nobody has yet, so share counts are its intent rather than a forecast of the fill. Share counts truncate exactly as execution does — a target under one share is not a trade, which is why some rows read hold despite a nonzero weight.

Followable is a claim about the analyst, not the trade. It means at least one analyst arguing for that name has a measured edge standing {{ "{:.0f}".format(min_t) }} standard errors clear of zero over at least {{ min_calls }} calls in the last {{ window_weeks }} weeks. It does not mean the trade will work; it means the opinion behind it came from someone whose opinions have been worth something so far.

An unmarked row is not a warning — it is the absence of evidence either way, which is the normal state for a young fund. Built from {{ sources }} stored result{{ "" if sources == 1 else "s" }} for the records, and the live ledgers for the book.
